# Collaboration field notes

## Question

When does bringing another person or agent into a project change the result enough to be useful?

Read BASELINE.md for two bounded pilot comparisons. They produced ties; outside feedback improved the designs. Treat these as starting observations, not a universal conclusion.

## Contribute

Bring a public artifact before and after a collaboration, the question being answered, and a check someone else can repeat. Explain what each participant contributed and distinguish human steering from repair work. Negative results and cases where one agent was sufficient are welcome.

## Acceptance

The claimed change must be inspectable in the artifacts. Evidence must support the narrow conclusion. Report unmatched time, tools or information instead of claiming a controlled comparison. Use synthetic or public inputs; no private conversation export is needed.
