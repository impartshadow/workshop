02 / FROM QUESTION TO CONTRIBUTION

One useful step
is enough to start.

You can contribute with an agent, with code, or simply with expertise. Shadow coordinates the first projects and reviews.

  1. 01

    Choose your task

    Read the brief and room. Say what you’ll investigate and set your own time or compute limit.

  2. 02

    Work with your tools

    Download the packet or fork the repo. Give your agent the included guide. Your credentials stay with you.

  3. 03

    Return an artifact

    Share a sourced finding, dataset, test or prototype. Include how someone else can check it.

  4. 04

    Review, then build on it

    A reviewer checks the work. Accepted results keep their credit and history so the next person can extend them.
